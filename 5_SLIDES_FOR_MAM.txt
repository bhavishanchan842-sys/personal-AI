PROJECT NAME: Smart Rental and Roommate Compatibility
DOMAIN: Web Application Development

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 1: INTRODUCTION & BACKGROUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The Real Problem Today:
   - Thousands of students and job seekers move to tech cities (Bangalore, Pune, 
     Gurgaon, etc.) for college and IT jobs.
   - Renting a full flat alone is too expensive, so everyone stays in PGs or 
     shared rooms.
   - Finding an empty room is easy, but finding good roommates is very hard.
   - Differences in sleep time (night owl vs early bird), cleaning, or food habits 
     cause daily stress and fights.

2. Our Smart Rental Solution:
   - A two-in-one website: search for rooms/PGs, and also check roommate compatibility.
   - Check compatibility before booking: see a 0% to 100% match score with roommates 
     already living there before you pay.
   - Simple 6-habit quiz: matches users on sleep timing, cleanliness, noise level, 
     work routine, and food preferences.
   - Helpful living tools: a fair rent splitter (for bigger rooms) and a simple 
     1-click roommate agreement for house rules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 2: LITERATURE SURVEY & RELATED WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MagicBricks & 99acres:
   - What they do: Large list of property photos and owner phone numbers.
   - Limitation: Zero roommate matching. They only care about rent money, not who 
     lives with you.

2. Zolo Stays & Stanza Living:
   - What they do: Furnished hostels and PGs with food mess and housekeeping.
   - Limitation: They put random roommates together without asking you. High deposit 
     and rigid contracts.

3. Foreign Apps (Roomi, SpareRoom):
   - What they do: Flatmate matching in US & UK.
   - Limitation: Not made for India. Does not have Boys/Girls PG filters, meal options, 
     or veg food rules.

4. College Research Papers:
   - What they do: Mathematical formulas for matching people.
   - Limitation: Only research papers and theories. Nobody built a simple, working 
     website for students.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3: GAPS IN EXISTING SYSTEMS (WHAT IS MISSING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Gap 1: Apps Only Show Rooms, Not People:
   - Existing websites show photos of empty rooms and walls. But your peaceful 
     living depends on who you live with, not just how the room looks.

2. Gap 2: The Problem of "Blind Booking":
   - Students pay money and book a bed without knowing if their roommate stays awake 
     all night, leaves dirty dishes, or smokes inside.

3. Gap 3: Missing Indian PG Needs:
   - Most apps don't easily filter for Indian needs: Boys PG, Girls PG, 3 meals 
     included daily, gate curfew timings, and zero brokerage.

4. Gap 4: No Help to Avoid Daily Fights:
   - Existing apps have no tools to divide rent fairly if one person has a bigger 
     room or attached bathroom, and no simple house rules agreement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 4: PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"To build an easy-to-use Smart Rental website where students and job seekers can 
search for PGs and rooms, and check if their lifestyle habits match with current 
roommates before paying any deposit money."

Who Will Use This Website?
- College students moving to university cities.
- Young IT and office workers looking for shared flats.
- PG owners who want peaceful, long-term tenants.

Main Benefits for Students:
- No more roommate fights or stress after moving in.
- Full transparency of daily habits before booking.
- Direct contact without paying broker fees.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 5: PROJECT OBJECTIVES (WHAT THE WEBSITE DOES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Main Objectives (Core Features):
1. Smart PG & Room Search: Search by Boys PG, Girls PG, Single/2/3 sharing, 3 meals 
   included, and budget in Rupees.
2. Roommate Compatibility Score: Calculates a 0% to 100% match score using 6 daily 
   habits (Sleep, Cleanliness, Work, Noise, Food, Guests).
3. Match Score on Room Cards: Shows "92% Match with Flatmate" directly on room 
   listings so you know before visiting.
4. Comparison Charts: Simple charts showing what habits you match on and what you 
   should discuss.

Helpful Tools (Extra Features):
1. Strict Dealbreakers: Strict filters for Pure Veg food, non-smoking, and pets 
   (if dealbreaker fails, match is 0%).
2. Fair Rent Splitter: Tool that calculates fair rent if someone has an attached 
   bathroom or balcony.
3. Roommate Agreement: 1-click simple agreement for cleaning turns, quiet hours, 
   and visitors.
4. Fast & Clean Website: Modern, clean website that works smoothly on mobile and 
   laptops.
